How solar panels work

By the Brightwave Solar team

Solar panels contain photovoltaic cells that generate direct current (DC) electricity when sunlight hits them.

An inverter converts that DC power into the alternating current (AC) your home uses. Any power you do not use right away can go to a battery or back to the grid, depending on your setup.

Output depends on panel orientation, shading, weather and the time of year, which is why a site-specific design matters.
