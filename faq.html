Frequently asked questions

How long does installation take?

Most residential installs take 1-2 days on site. Permitting adds 3-6 weeks before install.

Is the 30% federal tax credit available?

Yes for purchased systems, subject to current federal rules. Leases are not eligible. Consult a tax professional.

What happens in a power outage?

Only systems with a Vault battery keep power on during an outage. Grid-tied systems without battery shut off for safety.

What is your cancellation policy?

Full refund within 3 business days of signing. After that, a 10% restocking fee applies until permit submission.
